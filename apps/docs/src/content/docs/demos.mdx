## Why this matters

Each per-host `ComponentBinding` registered on top of `@atelier/components`'s baseline catalog is a local escape hatch — a place where the host has decided the LLM cannot be trusted to compose the shape from primitives. The **baseline-first pivot** (Wave M) collapsed 17 customs across the three demos to **0** by promoting the recurring shapes (`Queue`, `Logo`, `MetaBadge`, data-aware `Grid` + `Card` + `Gallery`) to baseline.

The `tests/marketplace-pressure.test.ts` gate caps each demo's custom binding count and ratchets it down. **Lowering the ceiling by deleting a binding is the only way to grow the catalog.**

> **Naming note.** "Marketplace" is overloaded in our codebase. Wave M's "marketplace pivot" is about the **catalog** (the product). The vault marketplace (V-6) is the **distribution channel** (`atelier://author/persona@version`). See the [ETHOS](/atelier/introduction/ethos/) for the full distinction.
